01 — Logo

The node-C mark

Six connected nodes forming a C around a solid core — systems connected around the core of a business. It echoes the workflow chains on the website (Hospital → Doctor → Patient → Appointment → Invoice), so the brand mark and the product story are the same idea.

Primary — on Canvas #FAF9F6

On Surface #FFFFFF (cards, documents)

On Ink #111827 (dark contexts — teal reads fine)

Wordmark lockup

Corewell Systems
Corewell Systems

Wordmark is typed, not drawn: Inter Tight SemiBold, tracking −0.02em, Ink #1B2430 (white on dark). Mark sits left of the name at roughly 1.6× the cap height, gap ≈ half the mark's width.

Usage rules

  • Clear space around the mark: at least 25% of its width on all sides.
  • Minimum size: 24px digital, 8mm print. It survives a 32px favicon — that's the test.
  • Teal #0F766E only, on Canvas, White, or Ink backgrounds.
  • No gradients, glows, shadows, outlines, or recoloring.
  • No stretching, rotating, or placing over busy photos.
  • No interwoven ribbon/knot styling — keeps clear distance from Corewell Health's mark.

02 — Color

Warm canvas, one accent

03 — Typography

Two typefaces, no more

Inter Tight for headlines (confident, tight tracking), Inter for everything else. Both free on Google Fonts — no licensing headaches for you or a designer.

Display — Inter Tight BoldHeadlines · tracking −0.02em
Heading — Inter Tight SemiBoldSection titles, card titles
Body — Inter Regular, 16px, Soft #57534E, line-height 1.6Paragraphs, captions
KICKER — INTER SEMIBOLD, UPPERCASE, TRACKED WIDELabels, eyebrows

04 — Voice

Outcome language, zero hype

Every caption and headline traces back to one line: "We design and build business software that solves real operational problems." Lead with what the buyer gets, never the tech stack.

Never saySay instead
We build websitesWe digitize businesses
React / Node.js developmentScalable business platforms
We know AWSReliable, secure cloud deployment
PortfolioProducts / Solutions / Case Studies
Cutting-edge, revolutionary, 10xPlain claims you can defend on a call

Boilerplate (bios, about sections)

Corewell Systems is a software design and engineering company building operational systems for healthcare, hospitality, education, and retail businesses. We design software around how teams actually work — not the other way around.

Hard rules (non-negotiable)

  • No invented client counts, revenue numbers, or testimonials — ever.
  • No client names or real product names in public content.
  • Demos and sample data are always labeled as demos and sample data.
  • Every post's call to action is Book a Consultation — one CTA, everywhere.

06 — Captions

Caption starters + hashtags

Structure every caption the same way the website argues: name the operational pain → show the outcome → one CTA. Short beats clever.

For a demo post (template C)

"Most software demos are a sales call wearing a costume. Ours is a login button. Pick a role — Admin, Doctor, Front Desk — and run a clinic's day on sample data. No signup, no pitch. Link in bio / comments."

#CustomSoftware #HealthcareSoftware #ClinicManagement #DigitalTransformation

For an article post (template D)

"The honest answer to 'what does it cost?' is a range and the reasons behind it — so we published both. What drives the number up, what keeps it down, and the five-year math against per-user subscriptions."

#SoftwareDevelopment #BusinessSoftware #SaaS #TechForBusiness

For a thesis post (template A)

"Every operation without a system pays an invisible tax: re-typed data, missing invoices, month-end reports built by hand. Custom software doesn't add capability on top of your business — it removes friction from inside it."

#BusinessAutomation #OperationalExcellence #SMB #CustomSoftware

Industry-specific (swap the accent color)

"Front desk, reservations, housekeeping, and reporting — one connected platform, one live picture of the property. Built for how hotels actually run." (Use the Hospitality amber #B45309 for this one.)

#HotelManagement #Hospitality #HotelTech #PropertyManagement
